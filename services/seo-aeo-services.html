SEO vs AEO

What are SEO & AEO services, and why you need both

SEO (search engine optimisation) helps your pages rank in search results. AEO (answer engine optimisation) helps your business become the answer that AI tools and featured snippets quote. Same foundations, different finish line.

SEO

Get ranked

  • Goal: appear on page one for the searches your buyers use
  • Shows up as: blue links, local results, product listings
  • Built on: technical health, keywords, content, links
  • Measured by: rankings, clicks, organic leads
AEO

Get cited

  • Goal: be named and quoted when people ask AI tools a question
  • Shows up as: AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, featured snippets
  • Built on: direct answers, structured data, entity clarity, trusted mentions
  • Measured by: citations, brand mentions, AI referral traffic

Google says its AI features rely on the same core SEO practices and helpful content as regular search, which is why we treat SEO and AEO as one programme. Read AI features and your website.
