What it is

What is Google Business Profile optimisation?

Google Business Profile optimisation, also called Google My Business (GMB) optimization, is improving every part of your free Google listing so it matches what customers search for, answers their questions, and shows Google your business is real, active and relevant.

It’s the foundation of local SEO: the work that helps you appear in the Google Map Pack, the three businesses shown beside the map, and in “near me” searches.

  • Right primary category
  • Accurate name, address & phone
  • Complete services
  • Clear description
  • Fresh photos & updates
  • Genuine reviews, all answered
  • Consistent details across the web
How Google ranks local results

Three things decide who shows up. Two of them we can improve.

Relevance

“How well a Business Profile matches what someone is searching for.”

We tune categories, services, description, attributes and your website so your profile clearly matches the searches you want.

You can influence this: a lot

Distance

“How far each business is from the customer who’s searching.”

No one controls where a customer searches from. We make sure your address or service area is set up correctly.

You can influence this: not really

Prominence

“How well-known a business is.”

Reviews and replies, photos, regular updates and consistent citations all build prominence over time.

You can influence this: a lot

Google also says: “There’s no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google.” That’s why we never sell guaranteed rankings. Source: Google Business Profile Help.
