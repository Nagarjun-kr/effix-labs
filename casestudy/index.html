From Invisible to Found

How we took a site from zero search visibility to 300+ Google impressions in four weeks, and then built Effix Labs to be found on Google, in online communities and in AI answers.

About This Project

Before Effix Labs worked on anyone else's visibility, we tested every method on our own. Our first site, effix.co.in, was a live test bed: we structured it, optimised it and tracked every change in Google Search Console. What worked there became the foundation of Effix Labs and of the way we work with clients today.

The Challenge

A new site with zero visibility, and three places to be found.

When the first site went live, nobody could find it. And Google is only one of the places people look now. They read Reddit and Quora threads before they decide, and they ask AI tools for recommendations. A business that is missing from any of the three loses customers it never hears about.

  • No visibility on Google: impressions were near zero
  • No keyword plan: pages weren't targeting any real searches
  • Weak structure: long blocks of text, unclear headings, few internal links
  • Nothing that AI tools or answer boxes could clearly read and quote

What We Did

Five focused areas. One clear goal: be found.

On-Page SEO

One clear H1 per page, a logical H2 structure, and a unique title and meta description written for the search each page should answer. Every page also got a canonical URL.

Content Structure

Long blocks became short, scannable sections that answer one question each. Written for people first, so search engines and AI tools can understand it too.

Keyword Planning

Research into what people actually search for, with each term used naturally on the one page that answers it best, instead of repeated across many.

Internal Linking

Related pages link to each other with descriptive anchor text, so crawlers find every page and visitors always know where to go next.

Structured Data & AEO

Schema markup that tells search engines who we are and what we offer: Organization, Service, Breadcrumb and FAQ data, plus direct answers that AI tools can quote.

What We Learned

SEO isn't a trick. It's a discipline.

  • SEO is more than keywords: structure, speed and clear answers all play a part
  • A well-organised page ranks better because it is genuinely more useful
  • AI tools reward the same things Google does: clarity, accuracy and consistency
  • Small, consistent improvements compound over time

Common Questions

Quick answers.

  • How long does SEO take for a new website? On our first site, impressions went from zero to 300+ in four weeks and 12+ keywords started appearing. Rankings and traffic usually take longer, and every site is different.
  • What is the difference between SEO and AEO? SEO helps a page rank in Google's results. AEO helps AI tools and answer boxes understand and quote it. Both depend on clear structure, direct answers and accurate structured data.
  • How can I check where my brand shows up? Run Effix Discover, our free visibility audit. It checks live Google results for your brand on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Quora and Threads, with no signup.
