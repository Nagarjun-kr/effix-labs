Frequently Asked Questions

Is the SEO audit really free?

Yes, completely. No hidden fees, no credit card, no strings attached.

What does the audit cover?

Technical SEO, on-page factors, keyword gaps, page speed, mobile usability, and backlink profile.

How long does it take to receive the report?

Within 48 hours of your submission.

Will you try to sell me something after the audit?

No. You receive the report with zero obligation. If you want help fixing the issues, we can talk — but only if you want to.

What makes EFFIX different from other SEO agencies?

No automated tools, no generic reports. Every audit is done by a senior strategist who actually reads your website and understands your business.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Typically 4–7 months for meaningful organic growth, depending on competition and your site's current health. Technical fixes can show impact sooner.

How do I get started?

Submit your website using the free audit form at the top of this page. No call required upfront — you'll receive the report within 48 hours.

What industries does EFFIX specialize in for SEO?

EFFIX works with startups, SaaS companies, local businesses, healthcare clinics, ecommerce brands, and service-based businesses looking to improve search visibility, organic traffic, and Google rankings through strategic SEO systems.

Do you provide technical SEO services for existing websites?

Yes. EFFIX provides technical SEO services focused on improving crawlability, indexing, Core Web Vitals, website structure, site speed, and overall search engine performance for existing websites.

Can local SEO help my business rank in Bengaluru searches?

Absolutely. Our local SEO strategies help businesses improve visibility in Bengaluru-based searches through Google Business Profile optimization, local keyword targeting, map-pack SEO, and location-focused content strategies.

What is included in a technical SEO audit?

Our technical SEO audit includes analysis of indexing issues, crawl errors, Core Web Vitals, website speed, mobile usability, structured data, internal linking, and overall search engine accessibility.

Do you optimize websites for AI search and AEO?

Yes. EFFIX focuses on modern search optimization strategies including AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), semantic SEO, and AI search visibility to help businesses stay competitive as search evolves.

How do you improve organic traffic growth?

We improve organic traffic growth through technical SEO improvements, search intent-focused content strategy, keyword architecture, internal linking optimization, and long-term authority building.

Is SEO better than paid advertising for long-term growth?

SEO creates sustainable long-term visibility by helping your business rank organically on Google, while paid ads stop generating traffic once campaigns end. A strong SEO strategy compounds over time.

Do you create SEO content strategies for businesses?

Yes. EFFIX builds SEO-focused content strategies using keyword research, topical clustering, search intent analysis, and semantic SEO to improve rankings and attract qualified traffic.

Why is Google Business Profile optimization important?

Google Business Profile optimization improves local search visibility, map-pack rankings, customer trust, and local traffic for businesses targeting customers in specific locations like Bengaluru.

How do I know if my website needs an SEO audit?

If your website has low organic traffic, declining rankings, poor Google visibility, slow loading speeds, or weak conversions, a professional SEO audit can identify the technical and strategic issues limiting growth.
