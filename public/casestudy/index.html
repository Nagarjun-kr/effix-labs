Case Study · Effix.co.in

SEO Optimization

How I built and optimized a personal website from scratch — and what it taught me about real SEO.

About This Project

Effix.co.in is my personal website — built as a live learning ground for digital marketing. I didn't just design it. I treated it as a real SEO project: structured it, optimized it, and tracked what changed. Every decision was intentional.

The Challenge

Starting with a blank slate — and zero visibility.

When the site first went live, it had no search presence. No one was finding it. The content existed, but it wasn't working for search engines or readers.

  • No visibility on Google — impressions were near zero
  • No keyword strategy — pages weren't targeting anything specific
  • Poor content structure — hard to read, hard to scan
  • Basic UX — layout didn't guide visitors anywhere useful

What I Did

Five focused areas. One clear goal.

On-Page SEO

Added proper H1 and H2 hierarchy across all pages. Optimized title tags and meta descriptions with targeted keywords to directly signal intent to search engines.

Content Structuring

Improved content readability by restructuring long blocks into shorter, scannable paragraphs with clear headings. Built for humans first, optimized for crawlers second.

Keyword Optimization

Conducted strategic keyword research and integrated terms naturally within high-value content areas. Maintained high readability while satisfying search intent.

Internal Linking

Implemented strategic internal linking between relevant pages. This distributed page authority efficiently and guided search crawlers smoothly through the site hierarchy.

UX Improvements

Refined the visual hierarchy and removed friction points. Enhancing the layout directly contributed to longer engagement times, sending positive quality signals to Google.

Results

Early signals. Real progress.

This is an early-stage project focused on learning and consistent improvement. The foundational optimizations have already yielded measurable, realistic SEO outcomes:

  • Impressions increased from 0 to over 300 within 4 weeks
  • 12+ keywords started appearing consistently in search results
  • Improved indexing and crawlability across core pages

Results with Data

Validated by Google Search Console

The strategic changes implemented are actively verified by data in Google Search Console:

  • Impressions growth: A clear, steady upward trend in organic visibility as Google registers the newly optimized structure.
  • Keywords appearing: High-intent queries are surfacing, directly matching the optimized H1s and meta tags.
  • Indexing improvements: Pages are discovered and indexed significantly faster due to the reinforced internal linking architecture.

Proof of Work

Visual Evidence

Transparency is crucial. Screenshots from Google Search Console demonstrate the upward trajectory in impressions growth, confirmed keyword visibility, and successful indexing improvements. This reflects the reality of an early-stage project focused on practical, consistent optimization rather than overnight miracles.

What I Learned

SEO isn't a trick. It's a discipline.

  • SEO is far more than just keywords — structure, UX, and content clarity all play a role
  • A well-organized page ranks better because it's genuinely more useful
  • Working on a live project teaches you things no course can — you see cause and effect in real time
  • Small, consistent improvements compound over time

Conclusion

Built with intent. Ready to apply.

This project gave me more than a portfolio piece — it gave me a real understanding of how SEO works in practice. I know what it takes to take a site from invisible to indexed, from cluttered to clear. This project reflects my ability to learn, implement, and improve SEO in real-time. I'm ready to apply these skills in a professional environment and contribute to measurable growth.
