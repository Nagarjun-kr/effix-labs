Transparency Policy

The SEO industry has historically suffered from a lack of clarity. EFFIX was founded on the principle of absolute transparency. We believe you deserve to know exactly how your investment is driving compound growth.

Unfiltered Reporting

We do not manipulate data to hide temporary setbacks or overstate successes. Our reports provide a clear, objective view of your organic performance, highlighting both the wins and the areas requiring strategic pivoting. We report on meaningful metrics—revenue, qualified traffic, and conversions—not just vanity rankings.

Methodological Clarity

We operate as an extension of your team. You will always know the exact tactics we are deploying on your website. We do not use "black box" methodologies, undisclosed private blog networks (PBNs), or algorithmic shortcuts that could jeopardize your domain's long-term health.

Direct Access

Our clients have direct, unrestricted access to their analytics dashboards, project management boards, and the senior strategists leading their campaigns. There are no account managers acting as gatekeepers; you speak directly to the engineers building your search visibility.

Conflict of Interest

We maintain exclusivity in certain high-competition local verticals to avoid conflicts of interest. We will never run competing campaigns for two directly opposing businesses in the exact same service area and geographic location.
